## Decision

Under Armour is the **buyer**. Select one real company **smaller than Under Armour** in a **different industry, not sportswear or apparel**, and explain how the acquisition wins customers and restores growth. Support it with public numbers and address deal economics, customer overlap, execution, governance, risk, and the alternative use of capital.

## Case logic to defend

1. The problem is weak demand and customer acquisition at full price, especially North America and footwear. Cost cutting alone does not fix it.
2. A target must provide repeat customer contact or distinctive distribution capabilities **and** be affordable to UA.
3. Added target revenue is inorganic; only measured incremental cross-selling, new buyers, and retained UA sales are a plausible strategic solution.
4. UA previously paid $475m for MyFitnessPal and sold it for $345m. This deal needs commercial integration milestones and a pre-agreed exit/stop rule.
